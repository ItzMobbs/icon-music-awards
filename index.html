ABOUT ICON

BUILT FOR THE CULTURE.

The ICON MUSIC AWARDS was created to give the city's music scene a stage worthy of its talent, ambition and influence.

001

MORE THAN AN AWARD SHOW.

ICON is a celebration of the artists, producers, performers, creatives and moments that shape the sound of Los Santos.

From breakthrough records to unforgettable performances, the awards exist to recognize the people pushing the city's music culture forward.
